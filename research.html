Research Projects

Human-centered AI and robot learning

HARL studies how robots can learn, adapt, and act intelligently in complex real-world environments, with particular emphasis on healthcare and human-centered applications.

Robot Learning

Imitation Learning & Visuomotor Policy

Learning robot behaviors directly from demonstrations and sensory observations for task-level automation.

Embodied AI

Vision-Language-Action Models

Research on connecting images, language instructions, robot states, and actions for flexible robot autonomy.

Simulation

Simulation-based Robot Learning

Physics simulation, synthetic data, and sim-to-real methods for efficient robot policy learning.

Medical Robotics

Robots for healthcare and intervention

Robotic Ultrasound

Robot-assisted ultrasound scanning with image guidance, force/compression control, learning-based navigation, and remote operation.

ENT & Swab Sampling Robotics

Automation and teleoperation for ENT examination and nasopharyngeal swab sampling using visual guidance and imitation learning.

Neuro-stimulation Robotics

Robotic systems for accurate and repeatable positioning of non-invasive stimulation devices.

Orthopedic & Image-guided Robotics

Robot learning and image-guided methods for alignment, insertion, and manipulation tasks in orthopedic procedures.

Intelligent Systems

Mechanisms, sensing, and control

Data-driven Kinematics

Neural-network-based forward and inverse kinematics for parallel and complex robot mechanisms.

Parallel Mechanisms

Stewart-platform-based mechanisms, compact master devices, and precision robotic motion systems.

Teleoperation & Visual Servoing

Human-in-the-loop robot operation, image-based feedback control, and intelligent sensing for remote robotic tasks.
